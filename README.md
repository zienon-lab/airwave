# AirWave · Touchless Interconnection

> No touching, no remote needed—a single in-air motion lets devices "meet" and connect with each other.

[中文](README_CN.md) | English

## Introduction
AirWave is the **"touchless interconnection"** module in the Zienon touchless gesture-interaction series. It uses a camera to recognize in-air motions such as waving, completing device discovery, pairing, and connection without contact, and can link with [AirMove](https://github.com/zienon-lab/airmove) / [AirTap](https://github.com/zienon-lab/airtap): connecting "connection" and "control" into one—first connect the device touchlessly, then control it touchlessly.

Typical scenario: the user makes a simple in-air motion to let a phone, TV, big screen, or smart-home device quickly establish a connection, eliminating remotes, QR codes, or tedious pairing.

## Core Capabilities
- **Gesture-triggered Connection**: A wave or similar motion initiates device connection
- **Contactless Pairing**: Eliminates remotes, QR codes, and tedious manual setup
- **Cross-device Interconnection**: Phone ↔ TV / big screen / smart-home devices
- **Linked with Gesture Control**: After connection, continue operation directly with [AirMove](https://github.com/zienon-lab/airmove) (cursor-level control), [AirTap](https://github.com/zienon-lab/airtap) (in-air tap)

## How It Works
1. Camera captures the user's motion;
2. Recognizes "connection intent" gestures such as waving;
3. Triggers the device discovery and pairing flow;
4. Establishes connection between devices (screen casting / content sync / control channel);
5. Seamlessly continues with AirMove / AirTap touchless control after connection.
